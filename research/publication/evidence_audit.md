**Limit of the weak number trace.** Many short decimals occur somewhere in the sources by chance: a random two-decimal number in [-0.1, 0.1] is always found, a three-decimal one 78% of the time and a four-decimal one 54% of the time (measured by the script). A pass of that check alone proves little; the interval trace and the in-memory re-analysis carry the weight. Section and DOI numbers are excluded from the trace as identifiers.
