# Claims audit checklist (M4)

Status: not started (M0). No claims exist yet.

- [ ] Every row of `research/claims_register.csv` has an evidence path that exists, or a verified external source.
- [ ] Every number in the manuscript, reports, demo and articles matches the raw results (denominators, units, precision, sample counts).
- [ ] Every simulated claim is labelled simulated and stays within the model's assumptions.
- [ ] No claim about real product quality, fraudulent reviews or commercial platform internals.
- [ ] Null, negative and failed results are reported.
- [ ] A second review pass challenged the preferred hypothesis (record who, when, findings).
